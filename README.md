## About Me

I am a cybersecurity researcher focused on penetration testing, vulnerability research, bug bounty hunting, and SOC operations. My work combines offensive and defensive security with a strong emphasis on practical labs, automation, and training. I build platforms, tools, and local lab environments to demonstrate real-world attack and defense techniques in a hands-on way.

- 🔍 Penetration testing, web security, and vulnerability research
- 🛡️ SOC workflows, SIEM monitoring, and incident analysis
- 🧪 Building labs, tools, and training platforms for security demos
- 🏆 Recognized for responsible disclosure to NASA, EC-Council, and LG
- 📍 Based in Kerala, India

- Secure training flow for beginners and intermediate learners

### 2) Enterprise-Grade SOC & Threat Monitoring Lab
